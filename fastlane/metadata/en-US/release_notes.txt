Welcome to Tables — our first release.

A calm, ad-free way for children to practise their times tables:
• Countdown — a timed race to answer as many as they can
• Revision — no timer, leaning toward the facts they find hardest
• Answer with multiple choice or a clean built-in number pad
• A mastery grid that tracks real progress, fact by fact
• Completely private — no login, and nothing leaves the device

Thanks for trying it. We hope your times tables practice feels a little calmer.
